
# Greys
### R.A.D.I.O. Universal Archives | Species Reference

**Common Name:** Greys  
**Homeworld:** Original homeworld exhausted  
**Civilization:** Nomadic, interstellar  
**Reproduction:** Cloning  
**Primary Communication:** Telepathy  
**Other Communication:** Verbal and written language  
**Social Organization:** Purpose-assigned clones  
**Spacecraft:** Saucer-shaped, unarmed scientific vessels  
**Technological Specialization:** Scientific research, observation, cloaking, and tractor beam technology  

## Overview

The Greys are a technologically advanced, telepathic species whose civilization is organized around cloning and predetermined individual purpose.

Having exhausted the resources of their original homeworld long ago, the Greys have become a nomadic civilization. Their population travels aboard spacecraft, sometimes gathering into flotillas rather than maintaining a permanent planetary homeland.

Every Grey is produced through cloning and assigned a specific purpose at creation. Although cloned, Greys develop individual personalities, preferences, and interests throughout their lives.

Grey society expects each individual to fulfill its assigned function. Those who deviate from their purpose are considered broken and are expected to submit to decommissioning and study.

The Greys are particularly notable for their scientific curiosity, telepathic communication, and advanced spacecraft.

## Physical Characteristics

Greys possess large, dark, almond-shaped eyes and slender fingers.

Their distinctive appearance makes them readily recognizable among the civilizations of the galaxy.

Further anatomical characteristics have not been established.

## Biology and Reproduction

### Cloning

Greys reproduce through cloning.

Each clone is created with a predetermined purpose, establishing the function it is expected to perform throughout its existence.

Despite their shared genetic origins, individual Greys develop distinct personalities. Experiences, relationships, and personal interests influence their development.

Consequently, clones may become psychologically different from one another even while sharing the same biological foundations.

### Individual Development

Personality development is a natural characteristic of Grey individuals.

Differences in temperament, curiosity, behavior, and personal preferences do not necessarily constitute defects.

However, these differences may eventually lead a clone away from its designated function.

Grey society distinguishes between individual variation and deviation from assigned purpose.

## Society and Culture

### Assigned Purpose

Purpose is a fundamental organizing principle of Grey civilization.

Every clone is created to fulfill a designated function. Individuals are expected to remain committed to that function throughout their lives.

A clone's assigned purpose determines its expected contribution to society.

### Broken Clones

Greys who deviate from their assigned purpose are considered broken.

Such deviation may develop gradually as an individual's personality, experiences, or interests lead them away from their intended function.

Broken clones are expected to voluntarily submit to decommissioning and study.

The study of broken clones allows Grey society to examine deviations from intended behavior.

### Rejects

Not every broken clone complies with the expectation of decommissioning.

Those who refuse may continue living independently of their assigned purpose.

These individuals are regarded as rejects and are highly stigmatized within Grey society.

A reject should not expect respect, acceptance, or recognition from other Greys.

### Individuality

Although Grey society is organized around assigned purpose, its members are not devoid of individuality.

Each clone develops a personality, and differences between individuals can be substantial.

The conflict between individual development and predetermined purpose is an inherent feature of Grey civilization.

## Homeworld and History

### Original Homeworld

The Greys originated on a planetary homeworld whose resources were exhausted long ago.

Their civilization eventually became nomadic, abandoning dependence on a permanent planetary homeland.

The original planet's name, location, and present condition have not been established beyond its historical exhaustion.

### Transition to Nomadic Life

The loss of their original homeworld as a sustainable center of civilization resulted in the Greys becoming a spacefaring, nomadic species.

Their civilization now exists without a permanent planetary homeland.

Spacecraft are central to their continued existence, providing the mobility necessary to sustain a dispersed interstellar population.

## Nomadic Civilization

### Life Among the Stars

Modern Grey civilization is primarily spaceborne.

Rather than concentrating their population on a single planet, Greys travel throughout interstellar space aboard their vessels.

Their nomadic existence distinguishes them from civilizations that maintain established planetary territories.

### Flotillas

Grey spacecraft sometimes gather into flotillas.

These gatherings bring multiple vessels together, allowing otherwise dispersed members of Grey civilization to congregate.

Flotillas are not necessarily permanent formations, reflecting the mobile nature of Grey society.

## Communication

### Telepathy

Telepathy is the preferred method of communication among Greys.

They can project thoughts directly into the minds of others, allowing them to communicate without speaking aloud.

Telepathic communication can convey information, questions, observations, and emotional nuances.

### Verbal Communication

Greys are physically capable of speech and can communicate verbally when necessary.

However, they generally consider speaking tedious compared with telepathic communication.

Speech remains an available means of interacting with individuals when telepathy is unsuitable.

### Written Communication

Greys also employ written language and digital recording devices.

These methods are used for documenting observations, preserving information, and interacting with technology.

## Intelligence and Behavior

Greys demonstrate sophisticated reasoning, curiosity, and the ability to study unfamiliar cultures.

They are capable of recognizing emotional responses in other species, although individual Greys may interpret or respond to those emotions differently.

Their behavior varies according to individual personality, experience, and assigned purpose.

No single temperament should be considered universal to the species.

## Technology

Grey technology emphasizes scientific observation, information gathering, and sophisticated methods of interacting with the physical environment.

### Spacecraft

Grey spacecraft are characterized by the classic saucer-shaped configuration commonly associated with unidentified flying objects, or UFOs.

These vessels are equipped for scientific exploration, observation, and research rather than military combat.

Their technology includes advanced cloaking systems, tractor beams, and extensive scientific instrumentation.

As a nomadic civilization, the Greys rely heavily on spacecraft for their continued existence.

### Cloaking Systems

Grey spacecraft possess advanced cloaking technology.

These systems allow vessels to conceal themselves during observation and other operations.

### Tractor Beams

Grey spacecraft are equipped with tractor beams capable of exerting controlled forces on external objects.

Tractor beams provide a means of interacting with objects beyond the spacecraft without requiring conventional physical contact.

### Scientific Instrumentation

Grey vessels carry extensive scientific instruments.

These systems support observation, examination, measurement, and the collection of research data.

Scientific capability is a defining feature of Grey spacecraft.

### Weapons

Grey spacecraft do not carry weapons.

Their vessels are designed around scientific and observational capabilities rather than offensive combat systems.

### Shipboard Controls

Grey spacecraft incorporate systems that can be operated through mental interaction.

Physical control interfaces are also present, providing an alternative means of operating shipboard systems.

## Research and Observation

Observation and documentation are established activities within Grey civilization.

Information gathered about other species may be compiled into reports and circulated among Greys.

Observers are a recognized role associated with gathering and interpreting such information.

The Greys' advanced scientific instruments and spacecraft support their ability to study other civilizations and environments.

## Relations with Other Species

### Terrans

Greys have demonstrated considerable interest in Terran behavior, particularly humanity's emotional relationships and social customs.

Interactions between the species have included observation, direct contact, and diplomatic or commercial negotiations.

Some Grey encounters with humans have involved captivity and the treatment of humans as pets.

These incidents should not automatically be interpreted as representative of every Grey individual's behavior.

### Saurenai

Greys and Saurenai participate in the wider interstellar community and have encountered one another in shared social and diplomatic settings.

### Virexians

Greys have interacted with Virexians through the wider interstellar community.

Their civilizations differ significantly in biological organization and social expectations.

## Galactic Society

The Greys are an established nomadic interstellar civilization.

Their spacecraft allow them to travel between systems, conduct scientific research, and interact with other civilizations without maintaining a permanent planetary homeland.

Individual vessels may travel independently or gather into flotillas.

Grey society places considerable importance on predetermined purpose, and individuals who abandon their assigned functions face severe social rejection.

Despite these expectations, every Grey develops an individual personality, and some pursue lives that depart from their intended roles.

Their civilization combines advanced scientific achievement, nomadic existence, and a social structure that places assigned function above personal freedom.
