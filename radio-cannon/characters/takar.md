# Takar

## Overview

A young Saurenai fungi farmer of the Ilvani Clan on Velocia. Takar shares the central narrative with Zahra, his longtime friend and farming partner.

## Physical Characteristics

His feathers are described as muted in color. The story describes him as having long, muscular legs.

## Personality and Behavior

Contemplative, quiet, diligent, and methodical. He takes meticulous care of the clan’s bioluminescent fungi and is respectful of clan traditions.

## Background

Takar and Zahra have worked together for years. Their shared routine includes morning clan rituals and tending fungi that provide food and light.

## Skills and Abilities

Care of bioluminescent fungi, soil and nutrient management, and maintenance of irrigation channels.

## Relationships

- **Zahra:** Longtime friend, fellow fungi farmer, and close companion with developing romantic feelings.
- **Feathered Sage:** An elder who leads the clan’s morning ritual and offers guidance.
- **Eldra:** Experienced fungi farmer who advises Takar and Zahra.
- **Korath:** Another young clan member whose attention toward Zahra causes Takar unease.

## Affiliations

Ilvani Clan.

## Canonical History

Takar identifies unusually dry soil in the fungi fields and suspects an irrigation blockage. He and Zahra trace and clear roots and debris from the water channel. Their close friendship carries feelings that neither initially voices openly.
