# Pluma
### R.A.D.I.O. Universal Archives | Character Reference

**Name:** Pluma  
**Species:** Saurenai  
**Homeworld:** Velocia  
**Affiliation:** Saurenai research outpost  
**Occupation / Role:** Junior Observer  
**First Listed Canonical Appearance:** Echoes of Stride-Star  
**Current Status:** Not established  

## Overview

A young Saurenai Junior Observer who works alongside Feathered Sage Aroth to investigate unexpected radio transmissions originating from Terra.

## Physical Characteristics

A vibrant orange crest; her feathers visibly respond to her excitement and curiosity.

## Personality and Behavior

Bright, inquisitive, enthusiastic, and intellectually capable, although less patient than Aroth.

## Background

Trained by leading minds on Velocia, Pluma earned a posting to the remote research outpost.

## Skills and Abilities

Scientific observation, data analysis, scanning and monitoring systems, and operation of organic research consoles.

## Relationships

- **Aroth:** Senior colleague, Feathered Sage, and lead researcher.

## Affiliations

Saurenai research outpost.

## Canonical History

Pluma assists Aroth in investigating Terra’s radio transmissions and scanning the planet. The pair misinterpret the importance of human footwear, devote substantial effort to its study, and attempt to reproduce shoes. They later compile their observations of Terran ingenuity.

## Canonical Appearances

- **Echoes of Stride-Star; A World of Death (also named in this work)**

## Current Status

Not established beyond the events depicted in the cited work.
