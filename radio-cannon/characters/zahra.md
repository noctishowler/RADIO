## Overview

A young Saurenai fungi farmer of the Ilvani Clan on Velocia. Zahra is one of the two central characters, working alongside her longtime friend Takar.

## Physical Characteristics

The story describes expressive feather movements and sparkling eyes, without establishing a precise coloration or measurements.

## Personality and Behavior

Lively, playful, expressive, observant, and dedicated to her work. She finds beauty in the glowing fungi and in the forest around the settlement.

## Background

Zahra and Takar have shared years of labor and friendship in the clan’s fungi fields.

## Skills and Abilities

Fungi cultivation, field care, attention to detail, and irrigation maintenance.

## Relationships

- **Takar:** Longtime friend, fellow farmer, and close companion with developing romantic feelings.
- **Feathered Sage:** Clan elder and guide.
- **Eldra:** Senior fungi farmer who offers advice.
- **Korath:** Fellow clan member who approaches Zahra and offers to show her his farming methods.

## Affiliations

Ilvani Clan.

## Canonical History

Zahra notices the obstruction in the irrigation channel while investigating dry patches with Takar. Together they clear the channel and restore the water supply. Their longstanding friendship carries increasingly apparent feelings.
