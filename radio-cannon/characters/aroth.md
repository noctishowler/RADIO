# Aroth
### R.A.D.I.O. Universal Archives | Character Reference

**Name:** Aroth  
**Species:** Saurenai  
**Homeworld:** Velocia  
**Affiliation:** Saurenai research outpost  
**Occupation / Role:** Feathered Sage; lead researcher  
**First Listed Canonical Appearance:** Echoes of Stride-Star  
**Current Status:** Not established  

## Overview

An older Saurenai Feathered Sage and lead researcher stationed at a remote outpost. He investigates radio transmissions from Terra with Junior Observer Pluma.

## Physical Characteristics

A crest of deep indigo feathers tinged with silver. Described as an older Saurenai still in service.

## Personality and Behavior

Experienced, analytical, initially cautious about the unexpected Terran signals, and capable of becoming intensely fixated on a research question.

## Background

Aroth is described as a direct descendant of the bio-engineers of Velocia. He has spent much of his life studying faint signals at a long-neglected outpost.

## Skills and Abilities

Signal analysis, scientific observation, research leadership, and operation of Saurenai organic technology.

## Relationships

- **Pluma:** Younger colleague and Junior Observer, accompanying Aroth on the Terra investigation.

## Affiliations

Saurenai research outpost.

## Canonical History

Aroth detects structured radio transmissions from Terra and organizes an investigation with Pluma. After discovering Terran footwear advertisements, the two become preoccupied with shoes and attempt to create footwear for themselves. Their investigation eventually leads them to acknowledge Terran ingenuity and compile a report.
